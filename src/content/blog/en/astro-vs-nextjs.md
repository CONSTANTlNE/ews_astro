Both frameworks are excellent, but they optimize for different things.

## Choose Astro when…

- Your site is mostly content: marketing pages, blogs, documentation
- You want the smallest possible JavaScript payload
- SEO and Core Web Vitals are top priorities

## Choose Next.js when…

- You are building a highly interactive, app-like product
- You need complex server-side logic on every request
- Your team already lives in React

## Our take

For most business websites, **Astro** ships less JavaScript, scores higher on Lighthouse and is easier to keep fast. We still reach for React islands when a feature needs interactivity.
