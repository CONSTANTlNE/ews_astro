Nordlight needed a storefront that felt premium on the slowest phone in the shop. We rebuilt the entire experience with static product pages and a lightweight cart island.

## Results

- **+42%** mobile conversion rate
- **0.9s** Largest Contentful Paint
- **100** Lighthouse performance score

## What we did

We designed a Georgian/English bilingual catalog, product structured data for rich results, and a checkout flow with Stripe. Everything is deployed on Cloudflare's edge.
